🔒 100% Local-First Architecture

Privacy Policy

Project Tandem — Household Financial Clarity | Effective Date: August 18, 2026

Executive Summary: Project Tandem is designed with privacy as its primary foundation. We collect zero personal data, run zero analytics trackers, connect to zero remote databases, and transmit no financial details over the network. All calculation data remains stored 100% locally on your device.

1. Data Collection & Processing

When you use Project Tandem (via web browser or Android application):

  • No Server Collection: We do not operate remote application backend servers. Your income inputs, expense line items, scenario overrides, and settings are never transmitted to us or any third party.
  • Local Storage (`localStorage`): All household state is saved locally inside your browser's isolated local storage (`project_tandem_app_v1`). You maintain total control over your data.
  • Client-Side Financial Engine: All ATO tax cuts, Medicare levy calculations, and cash flow formulas execute purely in your device's browser engine using JavaScript.

2. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Project Tandem contains no third-party tracking scripts, telemetry, or advertising software development kits (SDKs):

  • No Google Analytics / Firebase Telemetry
  • No Facebook / Meta Pixel
  • No Advertising ID tracking
  • No mandatory user registration or account creation

3. Data Storage & User Control

Because all data resides strictly on your local device:

  • Exporting Data: You can export your data at any time as a CSV financial summary report or JSON backup file via the in-app Backup & Export tool.
  • Deleting Data: You can erase all saved financial data at any time by clearing your browser cache or selecting Reset to Sample Data / Clear Cache inside the application.

4. Children's Privacy

Project Tandem does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone. Our application is a local calculation utility for adult household financial planning.

5. Changes to This Policy

If our privacy posture ever changes in future versions, this document will be updated immediately. However, our commitment to a local-first, zero-backend architecture remains fundamental to our mission.
